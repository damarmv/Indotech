# **App Name**: ServisPro Digital

## Core Features:

- Responsive Service Profile Display: Professionally present business details across all sections (Hero, About Us, Advantages, Footer), fully optimized for desktop and mobile devices.
- Interactive Service Package Catalog: Dynamic card-based display of all service packages, including illustrative images, comprehensive feature lists, transparent pricing, and integrated 'Pesan Sekarang' WhatsApp buttons.
- Project Showcase & Social Proof: A modern grid-style gallery for visual documentation of past work, complemented by a dedicated testimonials section featuring customer reviews and star ratings.
- Direct WhatsApp Communication: Seamless integration of prominent 'Pesan Sekarang' and main Call To Action buttons throughout the site to initiate direct communication with the business via WhatsApp.
- AI-Enhanced Service Copy Tool: An AI-powered tool designed to generate concise, persuasive, and SEO-friendly marketing copy for service package descriptions and headlines, enhancing customer engagement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme: A sophisticated dark theme with deep blues and crisp whites, conveying professionalism and modernity. The overall vibe is one of trustworthiness and precision.
- Primary color: A vibrant, moderately bright blue (#30ABE8) used for calls-to-action, active elements, and key highlights, providing a striking contrast against the darker background.
- Background color: A very dark, subtle blue-grey (#171A1C) serving as the main canvas, ensuring focus remains on the content and primary elements.
- Accent color: A light, energetic cyan (#47CFEB) for subtle interactive elements or secondary highlights, analogous to the primary blue but distinctly different in vibrancy.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, slightly techy aesthetic, adding impact to large titles and prominent text.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all other textual content, providing excellent readability and a clean, professional appearance across all screen sizes.
- Clean, line-art style icons for service advantages and feature lists to maintain a minimalist and modern look. Use solid, consistent star icons for customer ratings.
- A modern, minimalist layout emphasizing clean lines, ample negative space, and clear content hierarchy. Elements are presented in a well-structured and easy-to-navigate manner, optimized for both mobile and desktop responsiveness.
- Subtle and efficient micro-animations, such as smooth hover effects on cards and buttons, ensuring a dynamic yet fast-loading user experience without unnecessary visual clutter.
